# WAYFARER fidelity checklist

Compared with locked geometry, real-map whole/day, place-panel, large-ratio, and photo-sequence reference captures at 1440 x 900. The fictional sample differs in names, times and images.

- PASS: left WAYFARER wordmark, centered journey nav, right status, fixed 62px header.
- PASS: trip heading stays left when panel is collapsed; rail begins at the Day 1 banner.
- PASS: fixed right column with floating round map/detail cards, large map at 40% of column; current day emphasis and Whole trip map button.
- PASS: solid green centered rail with a parallel track of small round dots (not plus marks), alternating plain-title entries, quiet category/time bylines, no underlines, shared copper dot and arm for place, lodging, single photo and cluster.
- PASS: pastel inline Day N - date banner with overlapping small tag; continuing three-day fictional walk has a full start event, later pale paper chips, a parallel round-dot lane and final hollow diamond/time. Sample span is illustrative.
- PASS: transit is compact, with a solid teal rail leg, parallel round-dot track, a light paper-filled icon with thin border, bold small ink title, teal time/time-zone and teal origin to destination. A car replaces the train because the fictional trip drives.
- PASS: place photo fans retain three overlapping tilted images; single/cluster entries have one small tilted edge-to-edge photo card, with no white frame; place fans are also borderless.
- PASS: standalone paper notes have tape, timestamp, language-aware direction and alignment; English LTR/left, Hebrew RTL/right.
- PASS: place panel is title, inline date/time/tag, photos, note and Maps link. No eyebrow labels over titles, including photo, cluster and day panels.
- PASS: cluster panel has count title, time range, 2-column five-photo grid, bottom photo count and thin unframed chevrons; photo mosaics rebalance short pages, with pager visible without scrolling.
- PASS: fullscreen image overlay with navigation; no page or panel vertical scroll.
- FONT: the PNG references have no font metadata. The captured Latin shapes are consistent with Arial/Helvetica-style sans. Current CSS declares Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif; in this Linux capture Arial resolves to Liberation Sans. Entry titles are CSS 20px/750 (rendered bold), transit title 13px/700, small metadata 10px/500-700. Hebrew falls back to a locally available Hebrew glyph font. This is the closest evidence-based identification, not proof of the original font binary.
- LIMIT: replacement coastal sample photos differ visibly, include repeated assets, and some entries' prose does not match imagery. Live map tiles also vary with source availability. The reconstructed code is not the lost source bytes.

- PASS: exact page backdrop measured on three locked captures as RGB 255,253,250 (`#fffdfa`); the map/detail card interior measured RGB 255,253,248 (`#fffdf8`). After the final build, current screenshot pixels at (10,110), (800,110), (10,500) and (950,95) match those exact values.
